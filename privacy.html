Kingot: Gold Block Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Kingot: Gold Block Puzzle ("the app") is a falling-block puzzle game for Android published by Toolrivo ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your game stays on your device

Your levels, stars, scores, coins, unlocked themes, daily chest streak and settings are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no cloud sync. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless Android's own device backup restores it.

Language

The app reads your phone's language setting to show the right language. It does not use your location.

Game updates

To receive game updates, the app contacts the Expo update service (Expo, 650 Industries, Inc.). This request contains only technical information (platform, app version and the current update ID) and, as with any internet connection, your IP address. It contains no game data or personal information. See expo.dev/privacy.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, app interactions and diagnostic information. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

No selling of data

We do not collect, sell or share your game data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions: contact@toolrivo.com

Wir erheben, verkaufen oder teilen deine Spieldaten nicht.

Kinder

Die App richtet sich nicht an Kinder unter 13 Jahren.

Änderungen
